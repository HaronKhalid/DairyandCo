Privacy Policy

This is a starter notice for Dairy & Co. Please review it and update it to reflect your actual business practices and applicable requirements before publishing.

Information you provide

This website does not submit information to a Dairy & Co server. The contact form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. A message is sent only if you choose to send it in WhatsApp. If you build a basket order, the site places the selected product names, quantities, estimate, and the name or delivery address you enter into that WhatsApp message.

WhatsApp and external websites

When you choose to continue to WhatsApp, information in the message is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy practices. This website also links to Google Maps and social-network profiles. Those services may collect information under their own policies.

Payments and orders

This website does not accept online payment and does not confirm an order. WhatsApp messages are order requests. Dairy & Co must confirm product availability, any delivery details, and acceptance of an order directly with you.

Cookies and storage

The shopping basket is saved in your browser’s local storage so it can remain available on this device. The website does not send basket contents to a server. Clearing your browser storage removes the saved basket.

Contact

For a privacy question, contact Dairy & Co on WhatsApp at +92 319 9446974 or visit Raja Market (Merhbhan Market), Tench Bhatta, Rawalpindi, Pakistan.
